# Glossary

**Altman Z-Score** — A financial model predicting bankruptcy probability from five ratios.

**Beneish M-Score** — A model for detecting earnings manipulation in financial statements.

**DCF** — Discounted Cash Flow; a valuation method projecting future free cash flows.

**GICS** — Global Industry Classification Standard; an 11-sector taxonomy for equities.

**ICR** — Interest Coverage Ratio; EBIT ÷ Interest Expense.

**PIT Reconstruction** — Point-in-time reconstruction; rebuilding what data was available on a past date to prevent lookahead bias.

**Platt Scaling** — A calibration technique that maps a model's raw scores to probabilities.

**Sharpe Ratio** — (Portfolio Return − Risk-free Rate) ÷ Standard Deviation.
